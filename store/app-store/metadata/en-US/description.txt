Vstavka keeps a history of what you copy and shows it as cards in a panel at the bottom of your screen. Press Shift–Command–V, choose a card and press Return: the item is back on your clipboard, ready to paste with Command–V.

WHAT IT KEEPS
• Text, links, colors, images and files
• Cards show a preview and the source app when available
• The last 500 items from the past 30 days by default; you decide how many items to keep and for how long

FIND IT QUICKLY
• Search your history
• Filter by type: text, links, images or files
• Pin the items you use often; pinned items stay until you remove them
• Move between cards with the arrow keys, or press Command–1 to Command–9 to pick a card by its position

YOUR HISTORY STAYS YOURS
• History is stored only on your Mac and is encrypted before it is saved
• Vstavka has no accounts, ads or analytics
• Password managers are excluded by default, and you can add other apps to the list
• Pause saving before you copy something private, delete single items or clear the whole history

MAKE IT FIT
• Change the shortcut in Settings
• Light and dark themes with a choice of accent colors
• Interface in 24 languages
• Lives in the menu bar and can open at login

Good to know: text is saved without formatting. A file card keeps the path to the file, so the file has to be where it was when you paste it.

Vstavka is free and open source, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases.
